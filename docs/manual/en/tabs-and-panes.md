# Tabs and split view

## Tabs

Like a web browser, Arkha can have several folders open in one window, each in a tab with its own history.

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Open a new tab | **File → New Tab** or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd> |
| Open a folder in a new tab | Middle-click it, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>, or **Open in New Tab** in its context menu |
| Close a tab | Its close button, a middle-click on it, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>W</kbd> |
| Bring back a closed tab | **File → Reopen Closed Tab** or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd> |
| Go to the next or previous tab | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Page Down</kbd> / <kbd>Page Up</kbd>, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> |
| Go to tab 1 to 8, or the last one | <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> … <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>8</kbd>, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>9</kbd> |
| Move a tab | Drag it, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Page Up</kbd> / <kbd>Page Down</kbd> |

Drag a tab into another Arkha window to move it there, or out of the window to give it a window of its own.

Where new tabs go (after the current one in the order you opened them, right after it, or at the end), and whether the tab bar shows with only one tab, is set in **Preferences → Behavior → Tabs** and **Preferences → Display**.

**New Tab** opens in the same folder as the current tab. In **Preferences → Behavior → Tabs** you can have it open your home folder, the folder of the last closed tab, or a folder of your choice instead. A folder you open in a new tab (middle-click, **Open in New Tab**) is always shown there.

**File → Close All Windows** closes every Arkha window at once.

## Split view

Split view shows two folders side by side, each with its own tabs: handy for copying or moving files from one place to another.

- **View → Extra Pane** (<kbd>F3</kbd>) opens and closes the second pane. It opens in the same folder as the pane you're in; when it closes, the pane you're in stays and the other one closes with its tabs. Both can be changed in **Preferences → Behavior → Split View**: keep the other pane instead, and open the extra pane in your home folder, in the folder it showed when last closed, or in a folder of your choice.
- The menus and toolbar act on the pane you last clicked. <kbd>F6</kbd> switches to the other pane.
- **View → Swap Panes** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>U</kbd>) swaps the two sides, with all their tabs.
- **View → Same Location as Other Pane** goes to the folder the other pane shows.
- **Edit → Copy to Other Pane** and **Move to Other Pane** copy or move the selected items into the other pane's folder. Dragging them across works too.

With **Preferences → Behavior → Focus the pane under the mouse pointer** (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F3</kbd> turns it on and off), the menus and keys act on the pane the mouse rests on, after a moment you can set, without clicking into it first.
