# Arkha Manual

Arkha is a file manager: it shows the files and folders on your computer, on drives you plug in and on servers, and lets you open, copy, move, rename and delete them. It works much like Caja, MATE's file manager, and shares its bookmarks, notes, emblems and thumbnails with it.

This manual explains what Arkha can do and where to find it. Press <kbd>F1</kbd> in Arkha to open it.
